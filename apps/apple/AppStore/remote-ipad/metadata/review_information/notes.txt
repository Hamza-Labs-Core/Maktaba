Maktaba Remote is the admin app for a Maktaba home (a server a household runs for its own media). It needs an admin account on a home. For review, please use our review home's admin account, made for App Review only, on a home with public-domain and openly licensed content.

Home address: review.maktaba.tv
Username: {{REVIEW_HOME_ADMIN_USERNAME}}
Password: {{REVIEW_HOME_ADMIN_PASSWORD}}

How to sign in:
1. On the first screen, choose to type the home's address and enter review.maktaba.tv.
2. Sign in with the username and password above. The tabs show what needs attention, the queue, libraries, computers and the home.

A viewer account (not an admin) sees a screen that says this app is for the home's admins; that is expected.

What the permissions are for: Local Network finds a Maktaba home on the person's own network (Bonjour _maktaba._tcp). Camera scans a home's QR code or another device's sign-in code; it is asked for only when the person taps Scan.

App Transport Security: the app sets only NSAllowsLocalNetworking, for plain http to a home on the person's own network. Everything else is HTTPS.

Nothing is sold in the app, and it shows no prices or links to buy.

Maktaba is free and open source: https://github.com/Hamza-Labs-Core/Maktaba

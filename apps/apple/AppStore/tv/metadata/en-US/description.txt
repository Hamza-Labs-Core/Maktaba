Maktaba on Apple TV plays the films, series, lectures and audiobooks you keep on your own Maktaba home: the computer or NAS in your house that runs Maktaba. Nothing is sold in the app, and your library stays in your home.

To use it you need a Maktaba home. Maktaba is free and open source, from maktaba.tv.

What you can do
- Find your home on your network, or type its address, and sign in with a code from your phone.
- Browse your libraries, series and collections with the Siri Remote, and pick up where you stopped.
- Watch with subtitles in English and Arabic.
- Watch the live channels your home makes from your own library, with a guide.
- Profiles for each person, and parental controls your home enforces.

The whole app is in English and Arabic, right to left in Arabic. This app is for Apple TV and is available only from the App Store on Apple TV.

Your privacy
The app talks to your home. It sends nothing to us unless you choose to: see maktaba.tv/legal/privacy.

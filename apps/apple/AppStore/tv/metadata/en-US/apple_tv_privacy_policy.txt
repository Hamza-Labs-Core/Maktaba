Maktaba on Apple TV talks to your own Maktaba home, the computer in your house that runs Maktaba. It sends nothing to us unless you choose to. Crash reports go to your home only when you turn them on. A problem report goes to your home when you send one, and on to Maktaba only when your home's admin allows it and you tick "Also send to Maktaba"; names, titles, file names and network addresses are removed first and we keep it 90 days. When you watch a broadcast, Maktaba Cloud keeps your device's guest pass and counts playing minutes for the publisher's limits, for 7 days. No analytics or advertising.

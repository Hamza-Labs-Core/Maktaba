Maktaba Remote is for the person who runs a Maktaba home: the computer or NAS in your house that keeps your family's films, series and recordings. It shows what your home is doing and lets you look after it from your phone.

To use it you need a Maktaba home and an admin account on it. Maktaba is free and open source, from maktaba.tv. Nothing is sold in the app.

What you can do
- See what needs your attention, and try again a step that failed.
- Follow the queue: what your home is preparing now, and how long each job has run. Pause and resume a step.
- Scan a library for new files.
- Let a new computer join your home, with the six-digit code it shows.
- Approve a TV or another device that asks to sign in.
- Read your home's updates, its processes, its errors and logs, and the problem reports people sent you, and answer them.

English and Arabic
The whole app is in English and Arabic, right to left in Arabic.

Your privacy
The app talks to your home. It sends nothing to us unless you choose to: see maktaba.tv/legal/privacy.

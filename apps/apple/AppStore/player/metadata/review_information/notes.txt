Maktaba plays a household's own media from their Maktaba home (a server they run). For review, please use our review home, which carries only public-domain and openly licensed films, lectures and an audiobook.

Home address: review.maktaba.tv
Username: reviewer
Password: {{REVIEW_HOME_VIEWER_PASSWORD}}

How to sign in:
1. On the first screen, choose to type the home's address and enter review.maktaba.tv.
2. Sign in with the username and password above.

Parental controls: sign in as "reviewer-child" (password: {{REVIEW_HOME_CHILD_PASSWORD}}). The films for older viewers are hidden and "Ask a parent" sends a request to "reviewer".

What the permissions are for: Local Network finds a Maktaba home on the person's own network (Bonjour _maktaba._tcp). Camera (iPhone and iPad only) scans a home's QR code or another device's sign-in code; it is asked for only when the person taps Scan.

App Transport Security: the app sets only NSAllowsLocalNetworking, for plain http to a home on the person's own network (an IP address or a .local name). Everything else, Maktaba Cloud and a home's internet address included, is HTTPS.

Nothing is sold in the app, and it shows no prices or links to buy. The app streams video over cellular only from the person's own home; a test stream is any film on the review home above.

Maktaba is free and open source: https://github.com/Hamza-Labs-Core/Maktaba

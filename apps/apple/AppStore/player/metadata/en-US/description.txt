Maktaba plays the films, series, lectures and audiobooks you keep on your own Maktaba home: the computer or NAS in your house that runs Maktaba. Nothing is sold in the app, and your library stays in your home.

To use it you need a Maktaba home. Maktaba is free and open source; it installs on a Mac, a Windows or Linux computer, or a NAS, from maktaba.tv.

What you can do
- Find your home on your network, scan its QR code, or type its address.
- Browse your libraries, series and collections, and pick up where you stopped.
- Watch with subtitles in English and Arabic, made by your home if a film has none.
- Search your library by words, or by meaning when your home has it turned on.
- Watch the live channels your home makes from your own library.
- Listen to audiobooks and lectures with the screen locked.
- AirPlay to a TV, and keep watching in Picture in Picture.
- Set what each child in your house may watch, and how long, with parental controls that your home enforces on every screen.
- Reach your home away from it through Maktaba Cloud, if the person who runs your home has linked it.

English and Arabic
The whole app is in English and Arabic, right to left in Arabic.

Your privacy
The app talks to your home. It sends nothing to us unless you choose to: see maktaba.tv/legal/privacy.

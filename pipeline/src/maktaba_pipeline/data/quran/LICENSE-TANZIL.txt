surahs.json
    Surah names (Arabic and transliterated) and verse counts, taken from the Tanzil Quran
    Metadata (ver 1.0), Copyright (C) 2008-2009 Tanzil.info, licensed under Creative
    Commons Attribution 3.0. Source: https://tanzil.net

Maktaba uses the text to find which verse a video title quotes (display titles,
Story 27.11), and to compare the verses recited or quoted in a transcript with it (word
review, Epic 52, story 52.15): a transcript word that differs from the verse is shown to a
home admin with the verse's word, unchanged, as a suggestion, credited "Quran text:
Tanzil Project (tanzil.net)". Maktaba never alters the text: all normalisation is done in
memory.

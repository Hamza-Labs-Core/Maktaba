Maktaba on a NAS

Your media already lives on the NAS. Maktaba can live there too, with no other computer left running.

العربية

Maktaba runs on a NAS as one container: the server, the web app, live TV, the pipeline that reads your library, and its own database. It runs as an ordinary user of the NAS, never as root, and updates itself in a quiet window.

Choose your NAS

  • Synology: DSM 7.2 or newer, a Container Manager project filled in from your answers.
  • TrueNAS: Community Edition 25.04 or newer, an app.
  • Unraid: 6.12 or newer, a template from the Apps tab.
  • QNAP: QTS or QuTS hero with Container Station.
  • UGREEN: UGOS Pro with its Docker app.
  • ASUSTOR: ADM with Portainer from App Central.
  • Any other machine with Docker: a mini PC, a Linux server, Portainer or Dockge.

Will my NAS run it?

Maktaba picks a tier from the memory it may use: Light under 6 GB (under 8 GB on an ARM NAS), Standard under 16 GB, Strong from 16 GB. The tier only sets defaults, which you can change in Settings → This machine:

  • Light: speech to text off until you turn it on (then the base model, in the quiet hours), one video conversion at a time up to 720p.
  • Standard: speech to text with the small model, two conversions at once.
  • Strong: everything at full size, and the larger speech model (large-v3-turbo) on a processor with AVX2.

On Light and Standard, heavy work waits for the quiet hours (01:00 to 06:00), so the NAS stays quick for its own files. Setup refuses when Maktaba may use less than 3.5 GB: such a NAS serves best as storage.

Model Processor Video Memory (GB) With Maktaba

Your model is not listed? What counts is the memory and the processor. Maktaba checks both itself when it starts and says what it found in Settings → This machine. In brackets: the most memory the model takes.

Home, machine or storage

  • The home, on any tier: what every guide here sets up. Maktaba runs on the NAS, and phones, TVs and browsers watch from it. A computer with a graphics card can join it later and take the heavy work (After setup).
  • Storage only: the NAS keeps your media, and the Maktaba home runs on another computer that reads it from the NAS's shares. Nothing of Maktaba runs on the NAS. This suits a NAS with too little memory, or one already busy.
  • A machine of a home elsewhere: a Standard or Strong NAS can also join a home that runs on another computer, and take part of its work. A Light NAS is warned that it would slow down without helping much, and joins only if you choose so explicitly.

What to prepare

  • A user for Maktaba on the NAS, or the user that already owns your media folders. Write down its numbers (uid and gid): the guide for your NAS says where to find them.
  • Your media folders: the shares with films, series and recordings. Decide for each whether Maktaba may write there (subtitles and converted copies beside your videos) or only read.
  • A data folder on fast local storage, an SSD if there is one. Maktaba's database and thumbnails live there; never on a network share or a USB stick.
  • An internet connection during setup: it downloads the transcription and search tools (about 2 GB) and the speech models, once.
  • Its ports: Maktaba uses 8088 (the web app), 8080 (the apps), 8081 (live TV) and 54329 (the database). If another app on the NAS already uses one, Maktaba moves to a free port by itself, or you choose one (A port is taken).
  • About 20 minutes, and the NAS's address on your network.

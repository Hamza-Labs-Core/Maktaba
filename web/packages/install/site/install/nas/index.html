Maktaba on a NAS

Your media already lives on the NAS. Maktaba can live there too, with no other computer left running.

العربية

Maktaba runs on a NAS as one container: the server, the web app, live TV, the pipeline that reads your library, and its own database. It runs as an ordinary user of the NAS, never as root, and updates itself in a quiet window.

Choose your NAS

  • Synology: DSM 7.2 or newer, a Container Manager project filled in from your answers.
  • TrueNAS: Community Edition 25.04 or newer, an app.
  • Unraid: 6.12 or newer, a template from the Apps tab.
  • QNAP: QTS or QuTS hero with Container Station.
  • UGREEN or ASUSTOR: UGOS Pro's Docker, or ADM with Portainer.
  • Any other machine with Docker: a mini PC, a Linux server, Portainer or Dockge.

Will my NAS run it?

Maktaba picks a tier from the memory it may use: Light under 6 GB (under 8 GB on an ARM NAS), Standard under 16 GB, Strong above. The tier only sets defaults, such as how much work runs at once and the speech model. A NAS under 4 GB serves best as storage: run the Maktaba home on another computer, and it reads your media from the NAS.

Model Processor Video Memory (GB) With Maktaba

Your model is not listed? What counts is the memory and the processor. Maktaba checks both itself when it starts and says what it found in Settings → This machine. In brackets: the most memory the model takes.

What to prepare

  • A user for Maktaba on the NAS, or the user that already owns your media folders. Write down its numbers (uid and gid): the guide for your NAS says where to find them.
  • Your media folders: the shares with films, series and recordings. Decide for each whether Maktaba may write there (subtitles and converted copies beside your videos) or only read.
  • A data folder on fast local storage, an SSD if there is one. Maktaba's database and thumbnails live there; never on a network share or a USB stick.
  • About 20 minutes, and the NAS's address on your network.

Maktaba

Maktaba keeps your films, series and recordings in one library, on a computer or a NAS in your home. Everyone in the house watches on their TV, phone or computer, in Arabic or English.

Install Maktaba

Step-by-step guides for the computer that will run Maktaba. It updates itself after that.

  • On a NAS, such as Synology, TrueNAS, Unraid or QNAP
  • With Docker, on any Linux computer

If something shared through Maktaba's services uses your work without your permission, send a notice to our designated agent at copyright@maktaba.tv. We look at every notice, act on the valid ones, and tell the person who shared the material how they can respond.

## Our designated agent {#agent}

Hamza Labs receives copyright notices for every Maktaba service.

- Email: [copyright@maktaba.tv](mailto:copyright@maktaba.tv)

## What a notice must contain {#notice}

For us to act, your notice needs all of these. They are what the US Digital Millennium Copyright Act (17 U.S.C. § 512(c)(3)) and the EU Digital Services Act (Article 16) ask for.

1. Your full name, postal address, telephone number and email address. If you act for the copyright owner, say whom.
2. The work you say is infringed. If there are several, a list of them.
3. Where the material is: the address of each page on watch.maktaba.tv, and the programme and the time it played, if you know them.
4. Why you consider that it infringes your copyright.
5. A statement that you believe in good faith that the use is not authorised by the copyright owner, its agent or the law.
6. A statement that the information in your notice is accurate and, under penalty of perjury, that you are the copyright owner or authorised to act for the owner.
7. Your signature. Typing your full name is enough.

## What happens after a notice {#after}

- Your notice gets a number and goes to Maktaba's moderation team. We reply by email.
- If something is missing from it, we tell you what.
- When a notice is valid, we take the material down, usually within 24 hours. On a channel, the programme is replaced and does not play again.
- The person who published it is told what was taken down, why, and how to respond.
- Someone who sends clearly unfounded notices again and again can be barred from sending more.

## Counter-notices {#counter-notice}

- If your material was taken down and you believe it was a mistake, or that you have the right to share it, you can send a counter-notice.
- Sign in to Maktaba Cloud at [app.maktaba.tv](https://app.maktaba.tv/) and open your home's Broadcast page. The counter-notice is there, with the statements it must contain.
- We send a copy to the person who sent the notice. Unless they tell us within 10 to 14 business days that they have gone to court, the material can be put back.
- This website has no accounts, so it has no counter-notice form.

## Repeat infringers {#repeat-infringers}

Each valid notice that leads to a takedown is a strike against the publisher's account. A strike counts for 12 months. A successful counter-notice, or a notice its sender withdraws, removes its strike.

- **1 strike:** we tell the publisher.
- **2 strikes:** we warn the publisher that the next strike ends their broadcasting.
- **3 strikes in 12 months:** broadcasting ends for the account. Every channel goes off air, the Broadcaster plan is cancelled at the end of its current period with no refund for that period, and guest sharing is turned off. Reaching your own home from outside it is not affected.

To appeal, write to our designated agent at [copyright@maktaba.tv](mailto:copyright@maktaba.tv).

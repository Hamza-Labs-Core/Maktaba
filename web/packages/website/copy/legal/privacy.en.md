Maktaba runs in your home, and your films, series and recordings stay there. This website sets no cookies and counts no visitors. The apps send nothing to us unless you choose to send us a problem report. Maktaba Cloud keeps only anonymous totals, and deletes them after 90 days.

## This website {#website}

- It sets no cookies.
- It runs no analytics script and shows no advertising.
- It loads nothing from another website. The one exception is the copyright page, once it carries its notice form: that page alone then loads Cloudflare Turnstile, which tells a person from an automated sender. [Cloudflare's Turnstile privacy addendum](https://www.cloudflare.com/turnstile-privacy-policy/) says what Turnstile handles.
- Cloudflare serves this website. As for any site it serves, Cloudflare counts the requests to the whole site: how many, from which countries, and how many failed. These are totals, not a record of you. [Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/) covers what Cloudflare itself keeps.
- The install guides' forms keep every answer in the page. Nothing you type there is sent anywhere.
- The copyright notice form sends only what it asks for, to the place it names before you send it.

## The apps {#apps}

What Maktaba's apps send, and to whom:

- **To your home.** Your home is the computer that runs Maktaba, looked after by whoever set it up, not by us.
  - **Crash reports** go to your home only when you turn on **Send crash reports**. It is off until you turn it on, and you can turn it off at any time.
  - **Problem reports** go to your home only when you send one from **Report a problem**. The form shows you everything it will send first.
  - Before anything is sent, the apps mask sign-in tokens, email addresses, home folder names, card numbers and network addresses in it. No videos, messages or file contents are sent.
- **To Maktaba.** Nothing comes to us from the apps directly. A problem report reaches us, through your home, only when both of these are true:
  - your home's admin turned on **Let people send problem reports to Maktaba** (it is off until they do);
  - you ticked **Also send to Maktaba** on the form (it is unticked until you do).
- **To anyone else.** Nothing. No analytics or crash-reporting service is built into the apps.

When a problem report does come to us: before it leaves your home, names, titles, file names, folders and network addresses are removed, and you appear only as `user-1`. We see which home sent it, so we can answer, never who you are. We keep it for 90 days, and your home's admin can delete every report of the home at any time, with **Delete what Maktaba has**.

When crash reports are off, nothing leaves your device.

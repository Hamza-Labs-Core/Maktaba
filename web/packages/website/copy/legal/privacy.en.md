Maktaba runs in your home, and your films, series and recordings stay there. This website sets no cookies and counts no visitors. The apps send nothing to us unless you use Maktaba Cloud or choose to send us a problem report. The cloud's statistics are only anonymous totals, deleted after 90 days. A Maktaba Cloud account, if you make one, holds what it needs to work, and its emails go through Resend, a company in the United States.

## This website {#website}

- It sets no cookies.
- It runs no analytics script and shows no advertising.
- It loads nothing from another website.
- Cloudflare serves this website. As for any site it serves, Cloudflare counts the requests to the whole site: how many, from which countries, and how many failed. These are totals, not a record of you. [Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/) covers what Cloudflare itself keeps.
- The install guides' forms keep every answer in the page. Nothing you type there is sent anywhere.

## The apps {#apps}

What Maktaba's apps send, and to whom:

- **To your home.** Your home is the computer that runs Maktaba, looked after by whoever set it up, not by us.
  - **Crash reports** go to your home only when you turn on **Send crash reports**. It is off until you turn it on, and you can turn it off at any time.
  - **Problem reports** go to your home only when you send one from **Report a problem**. The form shows you everything it will send first.
  - Before anything is sent, the apps mask sign-in tokens, email addresses, home folder names, card numbers and network addresses in it. No videos, messages or file contents are sent.
- **To Maktaba.** From the apps directly, only what Maktaba Cloud needs, when you use it:
  - the email and password you type to sign in to a Maktaba Cloud account, and the calls that list and reach your homes;
  - a TV showing a sign-in code asks us for that code, with its platform and the app's version, and no account. We keep those and the TV's country: the code works for 10 minutes, and its record goes an hour later.
- **Problem reports to Maktaba.** A problem report reaches us, through your home, only when both of these are true:
  - your home's admin turned on **Let people send problem reports to Maktaba** (it is off until they do);
  - you ticked **Also send to Maktaba** on the form (it is unticked until you do).
- **To anyone else.** Nothing. No analytics or crash-reporting service is built into the apps.

When a problem report does come to us: before it leaves your home, names, titles, file names, folders and network addresses are removed, and you appear only as `user-1`. We see which home sent it, so we can answer, never who you are. We keep it for 90 days, and your home's admin can delete every report of the home at any time, with **Delete what Maktaba has**.

When crash reports are off, no crash report leaves your device.

## Your Maktaba Cloud account {#account}

You need no account to use Maktaba at home. If you make one, the list above says what it holds, for how long, and who handles it for us.

**Emails.** We write to you only about your account: to confirm your address, to reset your password, when someone tries to sign up with your address, and about payments and limits. We send no advertising.

- **Resend** delivers them. It sends them from Ireland and stores them in the United States: your email address, the email itself with its link, and whether it arrived. It keeps them for 30 days. [Resend's own page](https://resend.com/security/gdpr) says how it protects them.
- An address that could not be reached, or that marked an email as spam, stays on Resend's list of addresses it no longer writes to.
- The links stop working after an hour (a password) or a day (a confirmation), long before Resend deletes the email.
- The emails load nothing from anywhere, and neither we nor Resend track whether you open them or click.
- We keep an email's content, sealed, only until it is sent. For 35 days after that we keep its kind, time and outcome, with your address as a code made with a secret key, so nobody can flood your inbox. The network that asked for it we keep the same way, for a day.

**Bot check.** When it is on, the cloud web app's sign-in, sign-up and reset pages load Cloudflare Turnstile, which tells people from bots by your IP address and what your browser shows it, and our server sends it your IP address with its answer to check. The apps never load it. [Cloudflare's Turnstile privacy addendum](https://www.cloudflare.com/turnstile-privacy-policy/) says what it does with them.

**Deleting it.** Your account's page deletes the account: you are signed out everywhere, and it is erased 30 days later unless you change your mind. The security records in the list above stay.

## Children {#children}

Maktaba is your household's own server. This website collects nothing about anyone, children included, and what a child watches stays in your home.

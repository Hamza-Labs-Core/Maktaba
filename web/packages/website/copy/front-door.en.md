# The Phase 1 front door: copy (English)

Story 60.02 part B rewrites the few lines of the Phase 1 front door (`src/pages/index.html`)
in the voice of [voice.md](voice.md). Each `##` heading is a key; the Arabic is in
[front-door.ar.md](front-door.ar.md). The page takes this text once the owner has read
both languages; the home page (60.10) then replaces it in place.

## lead

Maktaba keeps your films, series and recordings in one library, on a computer or a NAS in your home, and plays them on every screen in the house, in Arabic and English.

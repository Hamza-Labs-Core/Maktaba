# Big screen

Big screen turns Maktaba into a full-screen view made for a TV or a big monitor across the room: large text, rows of pictures, and a player you can drive from the couch with a TV remote, a game controller, the arrow keys or a mouse.

## What it is {#what-it-is}

Big screen is another way to show the same Maktaba: your libraries, series, search and Live TV, with the same videos and the same watch progress. It works in the Mac, Windows and Linux apps and in a browser.

It has its own screens, along the top: **Home**, **Libraries**, **Series**, **Search**, **Live TV** and **Settings**. The look is always the dark library theme, whatever theme you chose for the normal view. You see only what you can see in the normal view: big screen shows the same libraries and channels your account is allowed.

## How to use it {#how-to-use}

### Open and leave big screen {#open}

- Click **Big screen** at the top of any page, or press `g` then `b`.
- In the Mac, Windows and Linux apps: choose **View → Big Screen** (`Cmd+Shift+B` on a Mac, `Ctrl+Shift+B` on Windows and Linux). The window goes full screen.

To leave, go to **Settings → Leave big screen**, or press Back on Home and choose **Leave big screen**. You go back to the page you came from, and the window leaves full screen.

### Start the app in big screen {#start-in-big-screen}

In the Mac, Windows and Linux apps, turn on **View → Start in Big Screen** (or **Settings → Start in big screen** inside big screen). The next time the app opens, it opens full screen in big screen. The setting is kept on this computer only.

### Move around {#move-around}

| To       | Keyboard             | Game controller             | Remote     |
| -------- | -------------------- | --------------------------- | ---------- |
| Move     | the arrow keys       | the D-pad or the left stick | the arrows |
| Choose   | `Enter`              | A (Xbox) or ✕ (PlayStation) | OK         |
| Go back  | `Esc` or `Backspace` | B or ○, or View / Share     | Back       |
| Search   | `/`                  | Y or △                      |            |
| Settings | the menu key         | Menu / Options              |            |

The item you are on has a gold ring. Left and right move along a row; up and down move between rows, and come back to the item you left in each row. Back returns to the item you chose before, not to the top of the page. In Arabic the screens run right to left and the arrows follow them. A mouse works too: point at an item and click; the pointer hides when you stop moving it.

### Home {#home}

The top of Home is what you were watching last, with **Resume** and **Details**. Below are your rows: **Continue Watching**, **On Deck** (the next lesson of each series you are in), **Recently Added**, **Recently Watched**, **Favorites**, the Live TV channels on now, and a row for each library you pinned (pin libraries on the [Libraries page](/library) in the normal view).

### Libraries and series {#libraries-and-series}

**Libraries** shows each library you can see, pinned ones first. A library opens with its rows (continue watching, recently added, most watched, its series) and then all its videos, newest first; choose **Load more** at the end for more.

**Series** shows every series. A series has **Continue** (the next lesson to watch), its seasons, and its lessons with a ✓ for each one you finished. Choosing a lesson plays it from where you stopped.

### A video's page {#video-page}

A video's page has **Resume** (where you stopped) or **Play**, **Play from the start**, its chapters (each plays from its start) the opening of its transcript (each line plays from there), **Mark watched** and **Favorite**.

### Search {#search}

Type with the keyboard on the screen, or your own keyboard. The **ع / EN** key switches the screen keyboard between Arabic and English. Results come as you type: **In titles**, and **In transcripts**, where choosing a result plays the video at that moment.

### Watch {#player}

While a video plays the controls hide. Any key brings them back.

- With the controls hidden: left and right jump back or forward 10 seconds (in Arabic, left is forward), `Enter` or `Space` plays and pauses, up or down shows the controls.
- With the controls shown: move to a button and choose it: −30, −10, Play / Pause, +10, +30, **Chapters**, **Subtitles** (a track or Off, and their size), **Audio** (when the video has more than one audio track) and **Next lesson**.
- Media keys play, pause and skip. On a game controller, X or □ plays and pauses, the shoulder buttons go to the next lesson or back to the start, and the triggers jump 30 seconds.

At the end of a lesson that you opened from its series, **Up next** shows the next lesson and starts it after 10 seconds, unless you turned that off in Settings or choose **Cancel**.

### Live TV {#live-tv}

**Live TV** shows each channel with what is on now. Choose one to watch it. In the player, up and down change channel, number keys go to a channel, `I` shows what is on next, and `Esc` goes back to the channels. More in [Watching Live TV](/help/watch-tv#keys).

### Settings {#settings}

- **Language**: English or العربية.
- **Play the next lesson automatically**: on or off.
- **Subtitles on by default**: shows a video's first subtitles when it starts.
- **Subtitle size**.
- **Start in big screen** (the apps only).
- **Leave big screen**.

These are kept in this browser or app.

## What the states and messages mean {#states}

- **Loading…**: big screen is reading the page from your Maktaba.
- **Something went wrong.** with **Try again**: Maktaba did not answer. Choose **Try again**.
- **Leave big screen?**: you pressed Back on Home. Choose **Stay** to keep watching, or **Leave big screen**.
- **Up next in 10**: the next lesson starts when the count ends.

## FAQ {#faq}

### Does big screen keep my progress? {#faq-progress}

Yes. It is the same Maktaba: what you watch in big screen shows in Continue Watching everywhere, and the other way round.

### Which game controllers work? {#faq-controllers}

Any controller your computer and browser see as a standard gamepad, such as Xbox and PlayStation controllers, by cable or Bluetooth. If one does nothing, press a button on it once: browsers wait for a press before they let a page use a controller.

### Can I use big screen without the app? {#faq-browser}

Yes. Open Maktaba in a browser and click **Big screen**. The browser may ask to go full screen; if it does not, press F11 (Windows, Linux) or `Ctrl+Cmd+F` (Mac).

### Can I manage the server from big screen? {#faq-admin}

No. Processing, users, libraries and server settings stay in the normal view. Leave big screen to reach them.

## When something goes wrong {#troubleshooting}

### The arrows do nothing {#arrows-do-nothing}

Click once anywhere in the window, so it has the keyboard, then try again. In a text field (Search) left and right move in the text: press down to leave it.

### The window did not go full screen {#not-full-screen}

In a browser, full screen needs a click or a key press first: press F11 (Windows, Linux) or `Ctrl+Cmd+F` (Mac). In the apps, use **View → Enter Full Screen**.

### A video does not play {#video-does-not-play}

The player shows **Something went wrong.** with **Try again**. Try again; if it still fails, see [Playing a video](/help/videos#play).

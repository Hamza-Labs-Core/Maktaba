# Playlists

Your own lists of videos, from any of your libraries, in the order you choose, played one after the other.

## What it is {#what-it-is}

A playlist is a list of videos you make: a study plan, the lessons for this Ramadan, talks to share with your family. It can hold videos from several libraries. Each user has their own playlists; nobody else sees them, not even an admin's pages.

Open [Playlists](/playlists) in the side menu (on a phone: **More**).

## How to use it {#how-to-use}

### Make a playlist {#make-a-playlist}

On the Playlists page, type a name under **New playlist name** and press **Create playlist**. The new, empty playlist opens.

### Add videos {#add-videos}

- On a video's page, press **Add to playlist**, then **Add** next to a playlist, or type a new name and press **Create and add**.
- On a library's **Browse** tab or on [All videos](/videos), press **Select**, check the videos you want, then **Add to playlist** (see [Select several videos](/help/library#select)).

A video already in the playlist is not added twice. New videos go at the end.

### A playlist's page {#playlist-page}

It lists the videos in order, with how long each one is and how far you got. Click a title to open the video's page.

### Play a playlist {#play-a-playlist}

- **Play all** plays the videos in order, from the first.
- **Shuffle** plays them all in a random order.
- **Play from here** on a video starts there and goes on to the end.

When a video ends, **Up next** shows the next one and starts it after 10 seconds. Press **Play now** to start it at once, or **Cancel** to stop there (see [Up next](/help/library#up-next)).

### Change the order {#reorder}

Use the up and down arrow buttons beside a video to move it one place. They work with the keyboard too. The new order is saved at once. The **✕** button removes a video from the playlist (the video itself stays in its library).

### Rename or delete {#rename-delete}

**Rename** lets you change the name; press **Save**. **Delete playlist** asks first, then removes the playlist. Its videos stay in your libraries.

## What the states and messages mean {#states}

- **No playlists yet**: you have not made one. Make one on this page or from a video's page.
- **This playlist is empty**: add videos from a video's page or from a selection.
- **Added 3 to Tafsir.**: the videos went into the playlist (the number counts only the ones that were not in it already).
- **Playlist not found**: the link points to a playlist that does not exist or is not yours.
- **Could not create the playlist**, **Could not add to the playlist**, **Could not change the playlist**: the server refused or could not be reached; the reason follows the message.

## FAQ {#faq}

### Can someone else see my playlists? {#faq-private}

No. Playlists belong to the account that made them. Another user who opens a link to one gets **Playlist not found**.

### A video disappeared from my playlist. Why? {#faq-hidden-video}

If an admin takes away your access to a library, its videos stop showing in your playlists (they are kept, and come back if the access does). A video removed from its library goes from the playlist too.

### How many videos can a playlist hold? {#faq-limits}

Up to 5,000 videos, and you can have up to 200 playlists.

## When something goes wrong {#troubleshooting}

### Playlist not found {#playlist-not-found}

- **What it means:** The playlist was deleted, or the link is someone else's.
- **Does Maktaba fix it itself?** No.
- **What you can do:** Open [Playlists](/playlists) to see yours.

### A change was not saved {#change-failed}

- **What it means:** The server did not save a new order, a removal or a rename.
- **Does Maktaba fix it itself?** No. The playlist stays as it was.
- **What you can do:** Reload the page and try again. If it keeps failing, check [System health](/help/system-health) or ask an admin.

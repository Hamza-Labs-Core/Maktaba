# Library

Find something to watch: Home, your libraries, each library's own page and every video in one grid.

## What it is {#what-it-is}

Maktaba watches your library folders and picks up new and changed files by itself; each new file then goes through [processing](/help/processing) (reading, transcribing, tagging) before it is fully ready. You browse what it found in four places:

- [Home](/): rows across every library you may see, like Continue Watching and Recently Added, Live TV on now, and a row for each library you pinned.
- [Libraries](/library): a card for each library, and the libraries you pinned in your order.
- A library's page: its own rows (**Overview**) and all of its videos with sort and filters (**Browse**).
- [All videos](/videos): every video of every library you may see, as cards or as a list.

Which libraries you see is set by an admin (see [Library access](/help/library-access)). A library you may not see does not show anywhere: not on Home, not in a count, not in a row.

## How to use it {#how-to-use}

### Home {#home}

Home opens when you sign in. At the top, a large **featured** card shows the newest series in your libraries, with **Play the first lesson** and **Open the series**, or a lesson an admin chose to feature. Admins choose it on a video's page with **Feature on Home** (press it again, or **Feature the newest series** on Home, to go back). If you cannot see the chosen lesson's library, you get the newest series instead.

Its rows scroll sideways: drag or swipe them, use the arrow buttons next to a row's title, or press Tab to move from card to card.

- **Continue Watching**: videos you started and have not finished (past the first 30 seconds, before the last 5%). The bar under a card shows how far you got.
- **On Deck**: for each series you are in the middle of, its next lesson after the last one you finished, with the series' name under the card.
- **Recently Added**: the newest videos across your libraries. New lessons of one series share a single card ("12 new lessons", with the newest lesson's picture) that opens the series; a lone new lesson shows its series' name under its card. **See all** opens [All videos](/videos) sorted by date added.
- **Recently Watched**: what you watched last, one card per video.
- **Favorites**: the videos you marked as favorites, newest first. **See all** opens [All videos](/videos?favorite=true) showing only them.
- **What's On Now**: the Live TV channels on air, when there are any (see [Watch TV](/help/watch-tv)).
- A row for each pinned library, in your order: what you are in the middle of there, then what is new. The row's title opens the library.

A row with nothing in it is not shown. A new home with nothing to show says how to start.

### Libraries {#libraries-page}

Each card shows four recent posters, the library's name and how many videos it has. Click a card to open the library's page. **All videos** at the top opens every video in one grid.

### Pin your libraries {#pinning}

Press the **pin** icon to keep a library at hand: it is next to the library's name on its page, and in the corner of its card on the Libraries page (it shows when you point at the card, and stays once pinned). The pin turns solid, a short message confirms it, and the library shows under **Pinned** in the side menu with its own row on Home. Press the solid pin again to unpin it. You can pin up to 20; with 20 pinned, the pin tells you to unpin one first.

The **Pinned** list at the top of the Libraries page sets their order: use the up and down arrow buttons beside each one (they work with the keyboard too). The side menu and Home follow the new order at once. Your pins are saved on the server, so they follow you to every browser.

### A library's page {#library-page}

The page opens on **Overview**. **Browse** shows every video of the library with sort and filters. Admins also see **Scan now** and **Manage**, which opens Manage libraries.

### Overview rows {#overview-rows}

- **Continue Watching**, **On Deck**, **Recently Added** (grouped by series) and **Recently Watched**, as on Home, for this library only.
- **Most Watched**: the videos played most in this library over the last 90 days, by everyone.
- **Series**: the series found in this library. Click one to open the series (see [Series](/help/series)).
- **By year**: one chip per year with how many videos are from it. Click a year to open **Browse** with that year's videos. The year is the file's date, which for downloaded videos is the day they were published.

### Collections {#collections}

A collection is a group of a library's videos an admin put together: the **Collections** row on **Overview** shows them, and **See all** (or **Collections** at the top of the page) opens the library's Collections page. Everyone who can see the library sees its collections; only admins make and change them.

- **A list**: the videos in the order the admin chose. Open it to play everything (**Play all**, **Shuffle**) or **Play from here**.
- **A saved filter** (marked so): it keeps the filters and sort of the **Browse** tab and always lists whatever matches them, as videos are added. **Show the videos** opens Browse with those filters.

Admins:

- **New collection** on the Collections page makes an empty list. To fill it, go to **Browse**, press **Select**, check videos, then **Add to collection**.
- On a list's page, **↑** and **↓** move a video (with the keyboard too), **✕** takes it out, **Rename** and **Delete collection** change the collection itself. Deleting a collection never deletes its videos.
- **Save as collection** on **Browse** saves the current filters and sort as a saved filter. **Edit filters** on its page opens Browse with them; change them and press **Save filters to the collection**.

### Browse and All videos {#all-videos}

**Browse** on a library's page and the [All videos](/videos) page are the same grid: the first covers one library, the second all of them. Everything you choose (sort, order and filters) is kept in the page's address, so Back, a refresh or a shared link keep it.

### Open a video {#open-a-video}

Click a card (or a title in the list) to open the video's page. See [Videos and the player](/help/videos).

### Sort {#sort}

Videos are listed **Recently added** first unless you pick another sort. Pick **Sort**: **Recently added**, **Recently changed**, **Date published**, **Title**, **Duration** or **Most played**. The arrow button next to it switches between **Ascending** and **Descending**. Each sort starts in its natural order: newest, longest and most played first, titles from A. While you search the grid, the default is **Best match**: titles that start with your words, then titles that contain them, then the descriptions, the most recently added first in each.

### Filter by year and by what you watched {#filters}

- **Year**: only the videos from that year. On a library's page you pick from the years it has; on All videos you type the year.
- **Watched**: **Not watched**, **In progress** (started, not finished) or **Watched**. This is about what you watched, not anyone else.

### Grid or list {#view}

Under **View**, choose **Grid** for cards with posters, or **List** for a table with **Title**, **Duration**, **Language** and **State**. Maktaba remembers your choice in this browser.

### Filter by language {#filter-language}

When the videos have a detected language, small chips with the language codes appear above the list: every language the library (or your libraries) has. Click one to show only that language; click it again to show all. **Clear filters** removes every filter (search, year, watched, language, tag, speaker, favorites, letter, length, added and resolution).

### Search a library {#search-library}

The search box at the start of the toolbar finds videos by words in their title or description: type and press **Search** (or Enter). On a library's page it searches that library; on All videos, every library. To search inside what is said in the videos (the transcripts), use [Search](/help/search).

### Filter by tag, speaker and favorites {#more-filters}

- **Tag**: only the videos with that tag. The number after each tag is how many videos have it.
- **Speaker**: only the videos where that speaker talks (the names an admin gave the voices Maktaba found).
- **Favorites**: only your favorites (press it again to show everything).

The lists only offer tags and speakers that this library (or your libraries) actually has.

### Filter by length, date added and resolution {#band-filters}

- **Length**: **Short** (under 15 minutes), **Medium** (15 to 60 minutes) or **Long** (over an hour). A video whose length is not known yet is in none of them.
- **Added**: the videos Maktaba added in the last week, month or year.
- **Resolution**: the picture's height: **SD** (under 720p), **HD** (720p), **Full HD** (1080p) or **4K** (2160p and up). A video not probed yet has none.

Each list only offers what the library has, with the number of videos after each choice. You can combine them with every other filter.

### Jump to a letter {#jump-bar}

When the grid is sorted by **Title**, a bar of letters shows above it: each letter that starts a title, Arabic and Latin, with **#** for titles that start with a number or a sign. Click a letter to show only the titles that start with it; click **All** (or the letter again) to show everything.

### Mark watched or not watched {#mark-watched}

On a video's page, **Mark watched** marks it as seen without playing it (say, a lesson you heard elsewhere). Press it again (**Watched**) to mark it as not watched; that also forgets where you stopped. A watched video has a tick (**✓**) on its card, and one you started has a bar showing how far you got.

### Favorites {#favorites}

Press **Favorite** (the heart) on a video's page to keep it at hand; press it again to remove it. Your favorites have a heart on their card, their own row on Home, and the **Favorites** filter on the grid. They are yours alone.

### Select several videos {#select}

Press **Select** above the grid. Each card gets a check box: check the videos you want (**Select all shown** checks every video loaded so far). Then:

- **Mark watched** or **Mark not watched**,
- **Add to favorites** or **Remove from favorites**,
- **Add to playlist** (see [Playlists](/help/playlists)).

Press **Done** to leave. Changing a filter or the sort clears the selection.

### Play all, Shuffle and Up next {#up-next}

**Play all** plays every video the grid lists, with its sort and filters, one after the other. **Shuffle** plays them in a random order (up to 200 at a time). [Playlists](/help/playlists) have the same buttons, and **Play from here**.

When a video ends, **Up next** shows what comes next and starts it after 10 seconds: the next video of what you are playing through, or, for a lesson of a [series](/help/series), the next lesson. Press **Play now** to start it at once, or **Cancel** to stay. What you play through is kept in this browser tab only.

### Load more {#load-more}

The grid shows 60 videos at a time. Press **Load more** at the bottom for the next ones. The number on the button is how many are shown now.

### Scan a library now {#scan-now}

Admins see **Scan now** on a library's page. It looks for new and changed files right away instead of waiting.

1. Press **Scan now**. **Scan started.** shows.
2. A progress bar shows **Waiting for a worker**, then **Looking for new and changed files**, with about how many minutes are left.
3. When it ends you see **Scan finished.** and the page reloads.

You can follow a scan, and every other step, on the [Processing](/queue) page. See [the scan stage](/help/stages#scan).

## What the states and messages mean {#states}

Cards only carry a badge when something needs your eye. A video still being processed has no badge.

- **Missing**: the file is no longer where Maktaba found it. See [File missing](/help/failures#file-missing).
- **Corrupted**: the file cannot be read as a video. See [Video could not be decoded](/help/failures#ffmpeg-decode).
- **Failed**: a processing step gave up on this video. Open the video's **Processing** tab, or see [Failures](/help/failures).
- **No audio**: the video has no sound track, so it cannot be transcribed. Nothing to do unless the file is wrong.
- **Replaced**: the file was replaced by a newer one in the library folder.
- **No videos yet**: this library is empty, or nothing in it has been found yet.
- **Nothing to watch yet**: Home has no rows yet. Open **Libraries**, or (admins) add a library under **Manage libraries**.
- **No libraries yet**: there is no library you may see. Ask an admin.
- **No videos match your filters.**: the filters hide everything. Press **Clear filters**.
- **Library not found**: the link points to a library that does not exist or that you cannot see.
- **Could not change your pinned libraries**: the server did not save your pins; they stay as they were. The reason follows the message.
- **Scan started.** / **Scan finished.**: the scan was queued, then ended.
- **Marked 5 as watched.** (and the like): your selection was saved.
- **Could not save the change**: marking watched or a favorite did not reach the server; the cards stay as they were.
- **Scan failed**: the scan stopped; the reason follows the message.
- **Could not start the scan**: the server refused to start it; the reason follows the message.

## FAQ {#faq}

### I added files to the folder. Why are they not here yet? {#faq-new-files}

Maktaba waits until a new file stops changing (for example, until a copy ends) before it adds it. Big copies over the network can take a while. If they still do not show, an admin can scan the library, or check the [Processing](/queue) page.

### Why do I not see a library my friend sees? {#faq-missing-library}

Each user sees only the libraries an admin gave them. Ask an admin (see [Library access](/help/library-access)).

### Why is a row missing from Home? {#faq-row-missing}

Rows with nothing in them are hidden. **Continue Watching** shows up once you stop a video part-way, and a pinned library's row once it has videos.

### Can someone else see what I pinned or watched? {#faq-private}

No. Pins, **Continue Watching**, **On Deck**, **Recently Watched**, what you marked watched, your favorites and your [playlists](/help/playlists) are yours. Only **Most Watched** counts everyone's plays, and it never says who watched.

### Does Maktaba move or change my files? {#faq-files-safe}

No. Maktaba reads your files; it never moves, renames or edits them.

### Why are some videos shown by file name? {#faq-file-name}

Until a video has a title, Maktaba shows its file name. [Finding metadata](/help/metadata) can give it a proper title.

## When something goes wrong {#troubleshooting}

### Scan failed {#scan-failed}

- **What it means:** The scan could not finish. The message names the reason, often a folder that could not be read.
- **Does Maktaba fix it itself?** The folder watcher keeps running and picks up files once the folder can be read again.
- **What you can do:** Make sure the share or disk is reachable, then press **Scan now** again. If the reason mentions a timeout or an unreachable share, see [NAS read timed out](/help/failures#nas-timeout).

### Could not start the scan {#scan-not-started}

- **What it means:** The server did not queue the scan, for example because you are not an admin or the library no longer exists.
- **Does Maktaba fix it itself?** No.
- **What you can do:** Read the reason after the message. Reload the page and try again, or ask an admin.

### Something went wrong. (the list does not load) {#list-error}

- **What it means:** The page could not read the list of videos from the server.
- **Does Maktaba fix it itself?** No, the page waits for you.
- **What you can do:** Press **Try again**. If it keeps failing, check [System health](/help/system-health) or ask an admin.

### The pin went back, or nothing was pinned {#pin-failed}

- **What it means:** The server refused the change (for example, the library was removed or you can no longer see it) or could not be reached; an error message says so. If you already have 20 pinned libraries, the pin says so instead.
- **Does Maktaba fix it itself?** No. Your pins stay as they were.
- **What you can do:** Reload the page and try again. If the library is gone, it drops off your pins by itself.

### A card has no picture {#no-poster}

- **What it means:** The video has no poster yet, or it could not be read.
- **Does Maktaba fix it itself?** Yes, posters are made during processing and appear once ready.
- **What you can do:** Nothing usually. If it stays blank, check the video's **Processing** tab.

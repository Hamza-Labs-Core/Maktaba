# Broadcast access

Who may watch the channels this home publishes to Maktaba Cloud. Viewers never pay and need no account. Only the home's owner and its admins can open this page or change anything on it.

## What it is {#what-it-is}

Each published channel is either:

- **Invite only** (the default): people watch with an invite link or a TV code you hand out.
- **Anyone with the link**: anyone who has the channel's link may watch. It needs the Broadcaster add-on and a verified account: a paid plan, a confirmed email, and 7 days since your first broadcast. The page lists what is still missing.

How many people may watch at once and how many viewer-hours a month come from your plan. A viewer already watching is never stopped by a limit; new viewers wait instead.

## How to use it {#how-to-use}

Open **Broadcast access** ([/admin/broadcast-access](/admin/broadcast-access)).

- **Access**: pick each channel's access.
- **Viewer limits**: how many devices on one network may watch an anyone-with-the-link channel (1 to 50; invited guests never count), and the minutes a day each viewer may watch (0: no limit).
- **Invite links**: give the invite a name, the channels it opens (or all), how many devices may use it and how many days it lasts, then press **Create invite link**. You get a link to send and a short TV code (MK-…) to type on a TV.
- Each invite shows how many devices used it. **Devices** lists them; **Remove device** stops one. **Withdraw** stops the whole invite: its viewers stop within a minute.

## What the states and messages mean {#states}

- **Active**: the invite works. **Expired**: its days ran out. **Withdrawn**: you withdrew it.
- **Up to N devices** instead of a count: Maktaba Cloud could not count the uses just now.
- **Too many devices on this network**, **The viewing limit is reached**: messages a viewer sees when a limit you or your plan set is reached. People already watching keep watching.

## FAQ {#faq}

**Do viewers pay or sign up?** No. They need only the link or the code.

**Does withdrawing an invite stop someone mid-programme?** Yes, within a minute: withdrawing is how you stop a viewer.

**Can an invite open a channel of another home?** No. An invite opens only this home's channels.

## When something goes wrong {#troubleshooting}

- **Link the home to Maktaba Cloud first**: the invites are kept in Maktaba Cloud; link the home in Settings.
- **Maktaba Cloud is out of reach**: try again in a moment. Viewers keep watching meanwhile.
- **Anyone with the link** is greyed out: the page says which item is still missing.

The same settings are in Maktaba Cloud's web app under **Who can watch**.

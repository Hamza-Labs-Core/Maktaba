What you get

Built for your home media library

Maktaba Cloud is a thin relay for the Maktaba server you already run at home. We don't store your videos — we just connect you to them.

Remote streaming, no port forwarding

Skip router config and dynamic-DNS hassle. Maktaba Cloud holds an outbound connection from your home server, so you can reach it from anywhere.

End-to-end encrypted

Sessions are TLS 1.3 with certificate pinning to your server's identity. We can't decrypt your video — even if we wanted to.

Smart relay routing

When direct connection works, we get out of the way. When it doesn't, we relay traffic through the nearest edge node — usually under 30 ms latency.

Family-ready

Family plan includes 6 user accounts with per-user history, watch progress, and parental controls — all sharing the same home library.

Push notifications

Get notified when new videos finish processing, when family members start watching, or when something breaks at home.

Bandwidth dashboard

See usage by device and per-user, including 4K stream quality, transcode counts, and monthly relay traffic — broken down clearly.

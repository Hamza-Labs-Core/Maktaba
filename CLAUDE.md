# Maktaba: notes for Claude sessions

1. **Read [`docs/claude-trail.md`](docs/claude-trail.md) first.** It holds the owner's standing
   rules, past decisions and why, failures and fixes, the live state, what is in flight and what
   waits on the owner.
2. **Before your session ends, append to it:** your decisions (and the options you weighed),
   what failed and how it was fixed, and any change to the live state or the waiting list. Add
   only; date every entry; cite real PR numbers or shas (`gh` / `git`), never guessed ones.
   Ship the update in your PR, or in a small docs PR of its own.
3. Never write secrets there or anywhere in the repo.

### Changed

- **Maktaba Player and Maktaba Remote are each one app for iPhone and iPad.** The iPad no
  longer has its own Maktaba Player for iPad and Maktaba Remote iPad: the same app from
  TestFlight (and later the App Store) runs on both, with the iPad's sidebar and wide layouts.
  An iPad tester installs Maktaba Player or Maktaba Remote, adds the home and signs in again:
  the old iPad app's saved homes, theme and playback settings don't carry over (what the home
  keeps does). Then delete the old iPad app, since until then iOS may open a `maktaba://` link in
  it. Apple's review turns down a second app that is the same app (guideline 4.3(a)).

### Changed

- **The changelog says which release brought each change.** On maktaba.dev/changelog, in English
  and Arabic, a change merged from now on is listed under the release that first contained it, the
  ones waiting for the next release on top. Earlier entries follow under their own heading, as they
  were written. For contributors: an entry is a pair of small files in `changelog.d/` instead of a
  line in `CHANGELOG.md`, so two pull requests never conflict on it.

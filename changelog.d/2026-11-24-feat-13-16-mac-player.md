### Changed

- **The Mac Player's sidebar in brass, and on the right in Arabic.** The Player for Mac marks
  the section you are in with a brass fill and brass symbols instead of the system's grey, with
  Profile at the bottom of the sidebar. Drag a section to reorder it or hide it from its menu;
  ⌘1 to ⌘5 follow the sidebar's order, and ↑ and ↓ move through it. A sidebar arranged in an
  earlier version stays as it was, and ⌘F opens Search even when it is hidden. A section keeps
  its place, its search and its pages while you are in another one. Profile's rows are the apps'
  own full-width rows with brass symbols in place of the system's buttons. Language in Profile
  now picks English, Arabic or the system's language in the Player itself: it asks first, in
  English and Arabic, saves what is playing (waiting three seconds at most for a home that does
  not answer), then starts the Player again in that language, and in Arabic the sidebar and the
  window's buttons move to the right. The iPhone and iPad apps are unchanged.

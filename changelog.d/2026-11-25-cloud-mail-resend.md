### Added

- **Maktaba Cloud sends its account emails.** Signing up, resetting a password and account notices
  now reach your inbox, in English or Arabic (your app's language, else your account's), from
  no-reply@maktaba.tv; reply to reach support@maktaba.tv. The mails load nothing from elsewhere and
  track nothing, and signing up or resetting a password answers the same for every address.

### Changed

- **The privacy page covers Maktaba Cloud accounts.** It says what an account holds and for how
  long, and that Resend delivers the account emails: sent from Ireland, stored in the United
  States for 30 days.
